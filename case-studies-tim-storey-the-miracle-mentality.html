How E2W Collective helped launch Tim Storey's book 'The Miracle Mentality'

The project

Harper Horizon, the HarperCollins imprint for books that strive to make the world a better place, hired Los Angeles Public Relations to run the public relations campaign for world renowned motivational speaker, author, and life coach Tim Storey's book, The Miracle Mentality, in March 2021.

Objective

To educate people about finding the tools inherent in the Miracle Mentality to help them lead more productive and satisfying lives, particularly during the challenges of the pandemic.

Strategy

Arrange for national and local talk show appearances for Tim to connect with hosts about the importance of utilizing the Miracle Mentality, and pursue online and print opportunities to spread the word about the book.

Results

Today Show's Hoda Kotb and Jenna Bush Hager interviewed Storey on May 18. People.com, March 2, 2021, highlighted Storey's conversation with Oprah about the Miracle Mentality. Jam T.V. featured him on their live April 5 show. He wrote a piece for Maria Shriver's Sunday Paper. He also discussed manifesting miracles and the art of comebacks with Maria Menounos on her podcast, Better Together.
