How do I choose the right PR firm? ▼

Check out the content and clients listed on the company website and see if any are similar to what your needs are. Arrange a call with the company owner or a high ranking person at the firm to determine if you are the right fit.

What is included in a PR package? ▼

Our work encompasses pitching broadcast, online and print media. Your monthly retainer fee will cover a broad range of pitching to strategic outlets which we will present to you before our work begins.

What placement can I expect? ▼

You can anticipate at least a few hits after the first month. It's impossible to say exactly how many since we never quite know what the response will be until we pitch.

How long does it take to get media placement? ▼

It typically takes at least four to six weeks to get our first hits since it takes time to reach our media contacts.

What is the PR process like? ▼

We interview you and anyone else involved in your campaign and build a blueprint of media outlets to pitch. We write the materials, including query letters and biographies, arrange photo shoots, and get everything out to the media. It typically takes about four to six weeks to see our first hits.

Can you get authors on talk shows? ▼

It depends on who the author is, but often we can. Self-help author Tim Storey appeared on the Today Show, Access Daily, and Hallmark Home & Family. The Zen Mama Guide authors, Teresa Palmer and Sarah Wright Olsen, appeared on the Kelly Clarkson Show and Good Morning America's third hour.

Can you help bloggers? ▼

Most definitely. Bloggers with a substantial social media following in particular can benefit from wider exposure through traditional media outlets by way of PR.

What should I ask my PR company? ▼

Ask what is realistic so there are no false expectations from the start. Also ask what they have accomplished with clients like you and whether they can share press decks with links to prior interviews.

Is hiring a PR firm worth it? ▼

Hiring a PR firm is a worthwhile investment. Third-party endorsements, unlike advertising, carry meaningful content for individuals and brands that resonates with audiences.

How much does it cost to hire a PR firm? ▼

Cost depends on your needs. The more media outlets being pitched, the higher the fee. Monthly retainers range from $3,000 to $20,000, with individual clients typically landing at the lower end of that range.

What should a PR firm do for you? ▼

A good PR firm generates enough interviews to compile a press deck of links to interviews across broadcast and print. That deck becomes a calling card companies can use to grow their business or brands can use to land endorsements.
