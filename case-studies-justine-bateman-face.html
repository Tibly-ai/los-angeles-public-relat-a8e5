How E2W Collective Helped Launch Justine Bateman's Book 'Face'

The Project

Justine Bateman and Akashic Books hired Los Angeles Public Relations to run the public relations campaign for Bateman's second book, published April 6, 2021: Face: One Square Foot of Skin. The book explores society's negative attitudes about women's aging faces, with Justine sharing her own experiences.

Objective

To shine the spotlight on the issue of women and aging and start meaningful conversations on the topic.

Strategy

Build a strategic media list spanning national broadcast, women-focused digital platforms, and high-end magazines. Seed strong pitches with those outlets around the need for the topic of aging to be discussed among women of all ages: why has plastic surgery and changing your face become the norm, and why can't women feel comfortable and free to age naturally today? Identify high-profile allies willing to add their voice to the conversation and coordinate joint appearances that would carry the message further than a single interview could.

"Elizabeth Much at Los Angeles Public Relations handled the launch of my book, 'FACE: One Square Foot of Skin,' and did an incredible job. She strategized for the book's target audience and hit the bull's eye. Her concentrated approach caused Amazon.com to sell out of the book twice within the first week of release, and caused outlets like Time Magazine and 60 Minutes Australia to knock on our door."

Results

Today Show kicked off the campaign on April 1, 2021 with a one-on-one interview with Hoda Kotb. Town & Country listed the book in their spring reading list. Tamron Hall Show, April 2, 2021: Paulina Porizkova joined Justine to add to the discussion of women and aging, a particularly challenging subject for women who made their living in front of the camera.
