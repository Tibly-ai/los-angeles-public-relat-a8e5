PR Events

Los Angeles Public Relations has tremendous experience handling the PR for Los Angeles based events. Our strategic PR includes media outreach before and after the event from traditional sources such as print, radio and TV, and virally online through blog communities and social networking sites, but extends much further than that.

Pre-event coordination also includes reaching out to potential corporate sponsors. Understanding the goals of an event, its audience, and its timeline lets us build a media plan that fits the occasion, whether it's a product launch, a charity gala, or a red carpet premiere.

We handle the details that make an event newsworthy: guest list strategy, press check-in, photo and video coordination, and follow-up coverage that keeps the story alive after the last guest leaves.
